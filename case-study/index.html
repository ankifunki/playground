Case Study — 001

Multi
Location

GlossGenius — Lead Product Designer

Opportunity

Apr – Oct 2025

GlossGenius was built for single-location salons and spas. Supporting multi-location businesses was the path upmarket, but every primary workflow assumed one location.

Not a feature. A new dimension across the whole product.

RoleLead Product Designer
ScopeLocations, staff, services, settings, calendar, clients

One location to many

Today

Every workflow assumes one business and one location.

Tomorrow

Every workflow has to work across many locations.

Core needs

Owner

How do I manage my overall business?

  • View all clients across locations
  • Track sales across locations
  • See analytics for the entire business

Operator

How do I keep running each location?

  • Book an appointment at a location
  • Edit my schedule per location
  • Manage inventory per location

Principle

Manage globally, with the flexibility to operate locally.

Information architecture

Current

Workflows sit directly under the business.

New

Locations become a layer between the two.

Switching locations

Option a

Global

One switcher for the whole app. No central view for owners.

Selected

Per workflow

"Team at Soho." Works for owners and operators at any number of locations.

Option c

Hybrid

Both, with conflicting logic and an extra step.

Shared patterns

  1. Set a default, override per location
  2. Assign people and services to locations
  3. "[Surface] at [Location]" switcher
  1. Bulk actions by default
  2. Pickers that scale to 20+ locations

Add a location

M1

A new location starts from an existing one: hours, services, staff and settings carry over.

Services

M2

Created once, offered per location, priced per member.

Staff invite

M3

Locations and per-location schedules are part of the invite, so new members are bookable on day one.

Team page

M3

"Team at [Location]" shows who works where, and when.

Settings

M4

Standard across locations, with a soft warning when one location drifts.

Calendar & clients

M6

Book at any location, with one shared client list.
